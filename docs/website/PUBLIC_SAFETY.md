# Cumulative Labs Public Website — Safety Rules

This repository is public. Source files, branches, commits, pull requests, workflow logs, generated output, and assets must all remain suitable for public release.

## The website may discuss

- The Cumulative Labs mission
- The idea of cumulative intelligence
- Why verified knowledge should survive
- The Explore → Challenge → Verify → Preserve → Improve philosophy
- The existence of a Private Research System
- The existence of League Vector
- League Vector as the first applied proving ground
- The five public principles
- Active research and development at a high level

## The website must keep private

- Internal technical architecture
- Internal prompts or agent structures
- Private review, handoff, memory, registry, governance, or verification mechanisms
- Private repository names or links
- Internal research reports, blockers, cycle information, evaluations, or benchmarks
- Model, provider, routing, data-source, credential, infrastructure, or security details
- Private roadmaps
- Internal system codenames

## Public-claim standard

Use factual, measured language such as “we build,” “we are developing,” “designed to,” “active research,” and “testing whether.” Do not claim proven autonomous improvement, superior intelligence, better forecasting, production-scale reliability, or commercial success without approved public evidence.

## Prohibited public material

- Secrets, tokens, credentials, API keys, or environment files
- Unsupported claims
- Fake metrics, customers, partners, investors, testimonials, team members, citations, or media coverage
- Raw production packages, font files, editor screenshots, or archival source files
- Unapproved product names or internal system codenames

## Review and deployment

All implementation changes require pull-request review. Public review must verify the copy, assets, metadata, and production output before deployment. Production deployment and custom-domain activation require explicit Founder approval.
