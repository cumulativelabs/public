# Cumulative Labs Homepage Refresh — Draft

Date: September 9, 2026
Status: DRAFT — not approved for production

## Goal

Keep the existing Cumulative Labs identity and "Intelligence That Compounds." positioning, while making the public site materially clearer, more concrete, and more credible. The visitor should understand within seconds:

1. Cumulative Labs is building AI research systems around durable, verified knowledge.
2. The private research system is the underlying research effort.
3. League Vector is the first applied product/proving ground.
4. The work is still in development; the site must not imply unverified capabilities or results.

Existing PUBLIC_SAFETY.md remains authoritative. Do not expose private architecture, prompts, agent structures, repositories, infrastructure, codenames, benchmarks, internal evaluations, or unsupported claims.

## Proposed page order

1. Hero
2. What We're Building
3. Why Cumulative Intelligence
4. Our Approach
5. Concrete Public-Safe Example / Product Preview
6. Principles
7. Contact / Closing

The main structural change is to move the work/product explanation immediately after the hero and reduce repeated philosophical copy.

## 3. Why Cumulative Intelligence

Label:
WHY CUMULATIVE

Headline:
GOOD WORK SHOULDN'T DISAPPEAR WHEN THE TASK ENDS.

Body:
Intelligent systems can produce valuable analysis, evidence, and conclusions. Too often, the next task begins without a reliable way to carry the best of that work forward. Cumulative Labs is exploring a different model: preserve what survives scrutiny, keep it traceable, and let future work build from it.

Contrast A:
STARTING OVER
Useful evidence is produced, reviewed, and then disconnected from what happens next.

Contrast B:
BUILDING FORWARD
Useful findings survive review, remain available, and can inform the next cycle of work.

Visual intent:
Use one strong before/after visual rather than several abstract paragraphs. Fragmented signals on the left should resolve into retained, traceable knowledge on the right.

## Visual direction

Preserve the approved public brand rather than copying the internal Cumulative OS console directly.

Keep:
- near-black / midnight foundation
- official nested-C mark
- Amber → Pink → Purple brand gradient
- large geometric display typography
- thin borders and disciplined cards
- restrained ambient motion
- generous negative space

Refine:
- make the hero more legible before decorative effects
- use fewer repeated atmospheric sections
- add one concrete process visualization
- add a real League Vector preview only when the underlying content is real
- reserve the strongest gradient/glow for hierarchy and calls to action
- make mobile composition a first-class design target

Do not add:
- fake dashboards or metrics
- simulated "live" activity presented as real
- heavy WebGL
- scroll hijacking
- autoplay video/audio
- cursor gimmicks
- excessive cyberpunk decoration that makes the company harder to understand

## Release gates

Before any production change:
- compare this draft against the live site and current source
- verify public-domain reachability and redirects
- verify desktop and mobile rendering
- verify keyboard navigation, focus states, contrast, semantic headings, reduced-motion behavior
- verify links and contact action
- verify canonical, sitemap, robots, favicon, Open Graph, and social preview assets
- check performance/Core Web Vitals where possible
- ensure primary content remains indexable
- run repository tests/build/lint/validation
- perform independent public-copy/privacy review against PUBLIC_SAFETY.md
- use PR review; do not deploy without Founder approval
