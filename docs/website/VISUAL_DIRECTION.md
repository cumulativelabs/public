# Cumulative Labs Homepage — Visual Direction

## Combined concept direction

The foundation homepage combines three approved directions:

- **Concept 10** defines the cinematic hero: dominant headline, large official nested-C symbol, luminous horizon, atmospheric particle waves, and a strong sense of scale.
- **Concept 1** defines editorial storytelling: asymmetrical composition, generous negative space, large narrative typography, and flowing visual fields balanced against concise copy.
- **Concept 9** defines structured information: thin-bordered cards, restrained gradient line icons, disciplined hierarchy, and controlled interaction states.

The overall experience should feel like the public entrance to a serious research lab: dark, premium, futuristic, ambitious, restrained, and slightly mysterious.

## Approved color system

- Amber: `#FF7A00`
- Pink: `#FF2D8D`
- Purple: `#8A2BE2`
- Midnight: `#081220`
- Slate: `#1E293B`
- Cloud: `#F8FAFC`

The primary gradient always moves Amber → Pink → Purple. Electric-blue undertones may support atmospheric depth but do not replace the brand gradient.

## Typography

Use a modern geometric system-sans presentation. Display headings are uppercase with controlled tracking and fluid sizing. Body copy remains mixed case, comfortable to read, and limited to sensible line lengths. No font files or remote font requests are used.

## Logo rules

- Use the official horizontal gradient artwork on dark backgrounds.
- Use the official balanced nested-C symbol without redrawing or altering its geometry.
- Preserve the approved gradient order and clear space.
- Do not stretch, rotate, crop, skew, reverse, or heavily shadow the mark.
- Use only the web assets required by the public site.

## Visual progression

The page tells a visual story in which scattered signals gradually become organized knowledge:

1. **Scatter** — the hero begins with exploratory particles and less-ordered wave paths.
2. **Explore** — mission visuals begin connecting signals.
3. **Challenge** — fragmented nodes are contrasted with retained rings and paths.
4. **Verify** — the five-stage approach becomes explicitly ordered.
5. **Preserve** — system orbits and evidence trajectories appear structured and traceable.
6. **Compound** — the closing field becomes coherent around the complete official symbol.

Each major section exposes a `data-visual-phase` marker so later scroll-linked effects can deepen this progression without restructuring the page.

## Motion boundaries

Motion is ambient and never blocks content. Approved motion includes subtle particle drift, wave-dash movement, orbit rotation, header state change, card lift, and intersection-based reveals.

Do not add scroll hijacking, cursor trails, autoplay video, audio, flashing, extreme parallax, mandatory intros, heavy WebGL, or movement that competes with reading.

All motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. The reduced-motion presentation retains the complete layout and visual hierarchy without animation.
